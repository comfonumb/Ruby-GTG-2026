The plan

TMC in the morning. UDS from the afternoon. Food, entertainment, old stories and enough time to actually catch up.

Important: Those coming only to TMC do not need to pay. They may contribute voluntarily if interested. All classmates visiting or staying at UDS are asked to contribute the additional ₹5,000 per person towards the common GTG expenses listed below.

Pricing

Clear separation between UDS food/room costs and the common GTG contribution.

TMC only

₹0
No payment required.
Voluntary contribution is welcome for the common event expenses.

UDS stay — couple

₹12,500
For a couple, including room and listed meals.
Lunch, tea/snacks, dinner on Sept 5, breakfast on Sept 6 + room rent.

UDS visit — no stay

₹2,000 / person
For visitors not staying overnight.
Dinner on Sept 5. The separate ₹5,000 common GTG contribution still applies.

Common GTG contribution — ₹5,000 / person

  • College alumni hall on Sept 5
  • Guest gifts and mementos
  • High tea at TMC
  • Video / photographers
  • Orchestra, sound system and DJ
  • Conveyance from college to UDS, if required
  • GTG logo T‑shirt
  • Other event expenses

How to pay

A few members of the organizing committee will contact the classmates allocated to them. The ₹5,000 common contribution can be paid directly to the contacting committee member.

For the UDS room/meal component, please follow the arrangement communicated by the committee.
